Ekiden — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Ekiden is a relay race management app published by Kanau Labs LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Ekiden has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers that receive your data. We cannot see anything you enter into the app.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — team and runner names, pace estimates, leg assignments, recorded exchange times, and race notes — is stored only on your own device. It stays there unless you choose to send it.

Sharing your race data

Ekiden includes an export feature that lets you send your race data to someone using your device's own email or share options. This only happens when you tap it. The exported file contains the runner names and times you entered. You choose the recipient; we receive nothing automatically.

During the 2026 beta, captains are asked to send this export to the developer so the app's time projections can be evaluated. This is voluntary and captain-initiated.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ekiden removes all of it from your device. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Ekiden is not directed to children under 13.
